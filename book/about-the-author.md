# About the Author

![Alessandro Bessi](../figures/generated/author.png)

Alessandro Bessi is an AI expert, data scientist, and software engineer
whose work spans machine learning, complex systems, and the
infrastructure required to make intelligent systems work in practice.
He holds a PhD in Economics and Social Sciences and a master's degree
in Statistics, and has conducted international research in computer
science at the University of Southern California. He is the author of
more than forty scientific publications, cited thousands of times, with
research covering misinformation, online social systems, and
computational behaviour.

Since moving from academia into industry, he has worked on production
machine-learning systems, AI products, cloud infrastructure, and tools
designed to support better decisions and human development.

Beyond AI and software engineering, Alessandro's interests include
chess, music, endurance training, and drawing. Each offers a different
way of exploring structure, adaptation, creativity, and disciplined
practice.
